### Context

In 2023 Galvanize launched a part-time Hack Reactor bootcamp to open software engineering careers to adults who couldn't quit their jobs: 38 weeks, 19 hours a week, live online, with a mastery-based model that let learners retake modules until they were proficient. After a year as a lead instructor teaching React, Django, and microservices, I was promoted to curriculum developer and given the program to own, building AI tools into it from the start.

### Approach

I used backward design: start with what a graduate should be able to build, write measurable objectives, then sequence units so difficulty builds rather than spikes. Every lab and assessment was revised each release cycle based on where learners got stuck.

### What I built

- A part-time TypeScript and Next.js program built around AI tools, from learning objectives through assessment
- Labs and assessments revised each release cycle based on where learners got stuck
- Contributions to the organization-wide AI curriculum rewrite, where I held a leadership role

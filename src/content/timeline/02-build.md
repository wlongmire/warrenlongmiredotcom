At the Children's Hospital of Philadelphia, I led development of game-based interventions for children on the autism spectrum and helped run the play sessions, where the games met the kids they were built for. At Penn's School of Nursing, I helped develop a PTSD training course for nurses, later featured in a 2014 White House fact sheet. At Little Bird Games, I built a learning platform for Duke University that helped 400 Chinese high school students prepare to apply to U.S. colleges, and a game suite for an ADHD study.
